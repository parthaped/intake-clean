# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

> **Critical.** Sign and date this **before** filing the provisional patent. The provisional should be filed in the name of the assignee (`[LLC NAME]`) — having the assignment in place at filing time avoids a later, separate USPTO assignment recordation and removes the largest single diligence issue investors and acquirers raise about solo-founder companies.
>
> **Recordation.** Within a reasonable period after filing the provisional (and again after filing the non-provisional), record this assignment, or a short-form notice of it, with the USPTO via the [Electronic Patent Assignment System (EPAS)](https://epas.uspto.gov). Recordation is constructive notice to subsequent purchasers under 35 U.S.C. § 261.

---

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of `[ASSIGNMENT DATE]` (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between:

- `[FOUNDER NAME]`, an individual residing at `[FOUNDER ADDRESS]` ("**Assignor**"); and
- `[LLC NAME]`, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal office at `[PRINCIPAL OFFICE ADDRESS]` ("**Assignee**" or the "**Company**").

Assignor and Assignee are referred to herein each as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Assignor is the sole creator of certain intellectual property relating to a software-as-a-service product known as **IntakeClean** and a multi-layer document-processing pipeline (collectively, the "**Project**").

B. Assignor is the sole member and founder of the Company.

C. Assignor desires to assign to the Company, and the Company desires to acquire from Assignor, all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to all intellectual property relating to the Project, in exchange for the consideration described herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and other good and valuable consideration described herein, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**"Assigned IP"** means, collectively, all of the following, whether now existing or hereafter created, that relate to or are useful in connection with the Project:

- (a) all software, source code, object code, firmware, scripts, build configurations, infrastructure-as-code, schemas, data models, and database content authored or compiled by Assignor;
- (b) all inventions, discoveries, improvements, methods, processes, techniques, algorithms, and know-how, whether or not patentable, including without limitation the multi-layer document intake pipeline described in [`01-invention-disclosure-memo.md`](01-invention-disclosure-memo.md) and [`02-provisional-patent-application.md`](02-provisional-patent-application.md);
- (c) all patent applications and patents (provisional, utility, design, plant, foreign and domestic), and all rights to file the same and to claim priority therefrom;
- (d) all copyrights and works of authorship, including without limitation all UI designs, illustrations, marketing copy, documentation, training data prepared by Assignor, and any other expression fixed in a tangible medium;
- (e) all trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos, brand elements, and goodwill associated therewith, including without limitation the marks `INTAKECLEAN` and any associated logo or wordmark;
- (f) all domain names registered by Assignor relating to the Project, including without limitation `intakeclean.com` and any variants;
- (g) all trade secrets and confidential information relating to the Project, including without limitation customer lists, prospect lists, pricing models, prompt templates, and operational metrics;
- (h) all moral rights, droit moral, and similar non-economic rights, to the maximum extent waivable; and
- (i) all rights to sue for, and remedies against, past, present, and future infringements of any of the foregoing.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Present Assignment.** Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys, and delivers to the Company, and the Company hereby accepts from Assignor, **all** of Assignor's right, title, and interest, throughout the universe and in perpetuity, in and to the Assigned IP. This is a present assignment and is effective immediately as of the Effective Date.

**2.2 Future-Created IP.** To the extent any element of the Assigned IP comes into existence after the Effective Date, Assignor hereby assigns it to the Company at the moment it comes into existence, without any further act of either Party being required. This provision is intended to satisfy the rule articulated in *Filmtec Corp. v. Allied-Signal Inc.*, 939 F.2d 1568 (Fed. Cir. 1991), that a present assignment of future-created IP is self-executing.

**2.3 Waiver of Moral Rights.** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Assignor waives any "moral rights" or similar non-economic rights in the Assigned IP, and agrees not to assert any such rights against the Company or any of its licensees, successors, or assigns.

## 3. Consideration

**3.1 Consideration.** In consideration of the assignment set forth in Section 2, the Company shall pay or provide to Assignor the following (collectively, the "**Consideration**"):

- (a) the issuance of, or credit toward, Assignor's membership interest in the Company as set forth on **Schedule A** to the Operating Agreement of the Company; and
- (b) **$10.00 USD** in cash, the receipt and sufficiency of which Assignor acknowledges; and
- (c) other good and valuable consideration as recited herein.

**3.2 No Other Compensation.** Assignor acknowledges that no royalty, residual, or other consideration is due to Assignor in respect of the Assigned IP beyond the Consideration described in this Section 3.

## 4. Representations and Warranties

Assignor represents and warrants to the Company that:

- (a) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP;
- (b) Assignor has full right, power, and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and to make the assignment set forth in Section 2;
- (c) the Assigned IP has not been previously sold, assigned, licensed, pledged, or encumbered to any third party;
- (d) to Assignor's knowledge, the Assigned IP, as of the Effective Date, does not infringe any patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property right of any third party, and Assignor is not a party to any litigation, claim, or threatened claim involving any element of the Assigned IP;
- (e) the Assigned IP was created by Assignor without the use of any confidential information of, or the equipment of, any prior employer; and
- (f) no third party has any joint authorship, joint inventorship, or other claim to any element of the Assigned IP.

## 5. Further Assurances

Assignor shall, at the Company's reasonable request and expense, execute any additional documents and take any additional actions reasonably necessary to perfect, record, register, or enforce the assignment of the Assigned IP, including without limitation:

- (a) executing assignment recordation forms for the USPTO, the U.S. Copyright Office, and any equivalent foreign offices;
- (b) executing any inventor's declarations or oaths required for any patent application;
- (c) cooperating with patent prosecution, trademark prosecution, and infringement litigation; and
- (d) providing testimony and evidence as reasonably required.

If Assignor is unable or unwilling to take any such action, Assignor hereby irrevocably appoints the Company and its duly authorized officers as Assignor's attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, to execute any such document and take any such action on Assignor's behalf.

## 6. Confidentiality

Assignor shall hold in strict confidence all confidential information of the Company comprising or relating to the Assigned IP, and shall not disclose, use, or exploit any such confidential information for any purpose other than the performance of Assignor's duties to the Company. This obligation survives indefinitely with respect to trade secrets, and for **five (5) years** with respect to other confidential information.

## 7. General Provisions

**7.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**7.2 Venue.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in `[COUNTY], New Jersey`. Each Party consents to personal jurisdiction in such courts.

**7.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Operating Agreement of the Company, constitutes the entire agreement of the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings.

**7.4 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

**7.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**7.6 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original, and by electronic signature.
