# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

> **Use.** Send this to investors, prospective customers, contractors, integration partners, and anyone else with whom you'll share something non-public, **before** the substantive conversation. It's "mutual" so it's symmetric — most counterparties will sign without negotiation.

---

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` (the "**Effective Date**") by and between `[LLC NAME]`, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal office at `[PRINCIPAL OFFICE ADDRESS]` ("**Company**"), and `[COUNTERPARTY NAME]`, located at `[COUNTERPARTY ADDRESS]` ("**Counterparty**"). Each is a "**Party**"; together they are the "**Parties**." Each Party may, at any time, act as a discloser of confidential information ("**Discloser**") or as a recipient ("**Recipient**").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss and evaluate a potential business relationship, which may include without limitation `[a potential investment / a potential customer engagement / a partnership / a contractor engagement]` (the "**Purpose**"). To do so, each Party may disclose to the other certain non-public information.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient — whether before, on, or after the Effective Date and whether in writing, orally, visually, or by any other means — that is identified at the time of disclosure as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes without limitation: source code, system architecture, prompt templates, model evaluations, business plans, financial information, customer and prospect lists, pricing models, product roadmaps, unannounced features, security practices, and the existence and substance of the discussions between the Parties.

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
- (a) is or becomes publicly known through no act or omission of Recipient;
- (b) Recipient lawfully knew before receipt from Discloser without obligation of confidentiality;
- (c) Recipient lawfully receives from a third party without obligation of confidentiality; or
- (d) Recipient independently develops without use of, or reference to, Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations

**3.1 Use restriction.** Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

**3.2 Disclosure restriction.** Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party other than Recipient's directors, officers, employees, contractors, attorneys, accountants, and (in the case of a Party that is an investor) prospective co-investors and limited partners (collectively, "**Representatives**") who (a) have a need to know for the Purpose and (b) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement. Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**3.3 Standard of care.** Recipient shall protect Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses for its own most-sensitive information, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**3.4 Compelled disclosure.** If Recipient is required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, Recipient shall (where lawfully permitted) provide Discloser prompt prior notice and reasonably cooperate with any effort by Discloser to seek protective treatment.

**3.5 No reverse engineering.** Recipient shall not reverse-engineer, disassemble, or decompile any tangible item embodying Confidential Information.

## 4. No License

Nothing in this Agreement is or shall be construed as a license, express or implied, of any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right. All rights remain with the Discloser.

## 5. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **two (2) years** (the "**Term**"). Recipient's obligations under Section 3 with respect to a particular item of Confidential Information continue for **three (3) years** after the disclosure of that item, except that obligations with respect to information that is a **trade secret** under applicable law continue for so long as it remains a trade secret.

## 6. Return or destruction

Upon Discloser's written request or upon termination of the Term, Recipient shall, within thirty (30) days, return to Discloser or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control, and certify the destruction in writing. Recipient may retain (a) one archival copy in its legal files for compliance purposes and (b) backup copies created in the ordinary course of automated backup systems, in each case subject to the continuing obligations of this Agreement for as long as such copies are retained.

## 7. No obligation to proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into any further agreement, to make any purchase, to make any investment, or to disclose any particular information.

## 8. Residuals

Notwithstanding Section 3, Recipient's personnel may use general knowledge, skills, and experience retained in their unaided memories as a result of exposure to Confidential Information, **provided** that this clause does not authorize disclosure of any Confidential Information, does not grant any license under any patent or copyright, and does not relieve Recipient of any other obligation in this Agreement.

> Investors will sometimes refuse to sign without a residuals clause; engineering counterparties usually will. If you remove this clause, expect more friction.

## 9. No warranty

Confidential Information is provided "**AS IS**" without warranty of any kind. Each Party disclaims all express and implied warranties, including those of accuracy, completeness, merchantability, and fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. Equitable relief

Recipient acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause Discloser irreparable harm not adequately compensable by monetary damages, and that Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive and other equitable relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the necessity of posting bond.

## 11. General

**11.1 Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**11.2 Venue.** Exclusive venue lies in the state or federal courts located in `[COUNTY], New Jersey`. Each Party consents to personal jurisdiction in such courts.

**11.3 Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings.

**11.4 Amendment.** Amendments must be in writing signed by both Parties.

**11.5 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's written consent, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to a successor in interest by merger, sale, or reorganization.

**11.6 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

**11.7 Counterparts; electronic signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
