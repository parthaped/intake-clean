# Independent Contractor Agreement

> **Use.** Have every developer, designer, illustrator, copywriter, or contractor sign this **before** they start work. If someone has already done work for IntakeClean without one, get a *backdated-acknowledgment* version of this signed retroactively — the IP-assignment provision is what protects the patent and any sale of the company.

---

This Independent Contractor Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` (the "**Effective Date**") by and between `[LLC NAME]`, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal office at `[PRINCIPAL OFFICE ADDRESS]` (the "**Company**"), and `[CONTRACTOR NAME]`, located at `[CONTRACTOR ADDRESS]` (the "**Contractor**").

## 1. Services

**1.1 Services.** Contractor shall provide the services described in **Exhibit A** (the "**Services**") to the Company.

**1.2 Schedule and location.** Contractor shall determine the manner, method, and means of performing the Services, subject to reasonable Company quality standards and deadlines stated in Exhibit A. Contractor is not required to work at the Company's facilities and is responsible for Contractor's own equipment.

**1.3 Independent contractor.** Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of the Company. Contractor is not entitled to any employee benefits, including without limitation health insurance, retirement contributions, paid time off, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance. Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes, withholdings, and reporting obligations arising from compensation paid under this Agreement, and the Company will issue Contractor an IRS Form 1099-NEC at year end if applicable.

## 2. Compensation

**2.1 Fees.** The Company shall pay Contractor the fees set forth in Exhibit A.

**2.2 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Contractor for reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses upon receipt of supporting documentation.

**2.3 Invoicing.** Contractor shall invoice the Company monthly. The Company shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## 3. Intellectual Property

**3.1 Work Product.** "**Work Product**" means all deliverables, materials, code, designs, drafts, sketches, configurations, documentation, data, and other works of authorship or invention created or developed by Contractor, alone or with others, in the course of performing the Services or using any Company resources or confidential information.

**3.2 Work-for-Hire.** All Work Product is "work made for hire" within the meaning of 17 U.S.C. § 101 to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, and the Company is the author and exclusive owner of all such Work Product.

**3.3 Present Assignment.** To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as "work made for hire," or to the extent any rights would otherwise vest in Contractor, Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company, throughout the universe and in perpetuity, all of Contractor's right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all patent rights, copyrights, trademark rights, trade-secret rights, moral rights (waived to the maximum extent permitted), and all causes of action and remedies for past, present, and future infringement.

**3.4 Future Improvements.** The assignment in Section 3.3 covers any improvements, modifications, or derivative works of the Work Product subsequently created by Contractor, regardless of whether they are created during or after the term of this Agreement, to the extent they are based on, or use, Company confidential information.

**3.5 Pre-existing Materials.** If Contractor incorporates into any Work Product any pre-existing material owned by Contractor or by a third party (e.g., open-source libraries, prior personal projects), Contractor shall (a) identify each such item and its license terms in writing to the Company before delivery, and (b) grant (or procure) a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable license to the Company to use, modify, distribute, and sublicense that material as part of the Work Product. Contractor shall not incorporate any pre-existing material that is licensed under terms incompatible with the Company's commercial distribution of the Work Product (e.g., AGPL, SSPL) without the Company's prior written consent.

**3.6 Further Assurances.** Contractor shall execute any documents and take any actions reasonably requested by the Company to perfect, record, register, or enforce the Company's rights in the Work Product, and irrevocably appoints the Company as Contractor's attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, to do so on Contractor's behalf if Contractor is unavailable or unwilling.

## 4. Confidentiality

**4.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by the Company to Contractor, whether written, oral, visual, or in any other form, including without limitation source code, business plans, customer lists, prospect lists, prompt templates, model evaluations, financial information, and any client or end-client documents Contractor may access.

**4.2 Obligations.** Contractor shall (a) use Confidential Information solely to perform the Services; (b) protect it with at least the degree of care Contractor uses for its own most-sensitive information, and in no event less than reasonable care; and (c) not disclose it to any third party without the Company's prior written consent.

**4.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (i) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of Contractor; (ii) Contractor lawfully knew before disclosure by the Company without obligation of confidentiality; (iii) Contractor lawfully receives from a third party without obligation of confidentiality; or (iv) Contractor independently develops without use of Confidential Information.

**4.4 Compelled Disclosure.** If Contractor is compelled by law or court order to disclose Confidential Information, Contractor shall provide prompt notice to the Company (where lawfully permitted) and reasonably cooperate with any effort by the Company to seek protective treatment.

**4.5 End-client Data.** Contractor acknowledges that the Company's business involves attorney-client and end-client documents, that this material may include personally identifiable information, and that Contractor will treat all such material as Confidential Information of the highest sensitivity. Contractor shall not store any such material on personal devices or transmit it to any third-party service (including AI tools, chatbots, or paste-bins) without the Company's prior written consent.

**4.6 Survival.** Sections 4.1–4.5 survive termination of this Agreement for **five (5) years** with respect to non-trade-secret Confidential Information, and indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

## 5. Restrictive Covenants

**5.1 Non-solicitation of Customers.** During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** thereafter, Contractor shall not solicit any then-customer of the Company that Contractor learned of through the engagement to terminate or reduce its relationship with the Company.

**5.2 Non-solicitation of Personnel.** During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** thereafter, Contractor shall not solicit any then-employee or contractor of the Company to terminate their relationship with the Company.

**5.3 No Non-Compete.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Contractor from working for any other party in any field, subject only to Contractor's confidentiality and IP-assignment obligations herein. New Jersey courts disfavor broad non-competes against contractors; this Agreement intentionally omits one.

## 6. Representations and Warranties

Contractor represents and warrants to the Company that (a) Contractor has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement; (b) entering into this Agreement does not breach any other agreement to which Contractor is bound; (c) the Work Product will be Contractor's original work and will not infringe any third-party right (subject to Section 3.5); and (d) the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

## 7. Indemnification

Contractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Company against any third-party claim arising from (a) Contractor's breach of Sections 3, 4, or 6, or (b) Contractor's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services.

## 8. Term and Termination

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the Services are completed, unless earlier terminated.

**8.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on **fourteen (14) days'** written notice. The Company shall pay Contractor for Services performed through the effective date of termination.

**8.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within ten (10) days of notice.

**8.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Contractor shall (a) deliver all Work Product in progress; (b) return or destroy all Confidential Information; and (c) cooperate with reasonable transition activities. Sections 3, 4, 5, 7, and 9 survive termination.

## 9. General

**9.1 Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Exclusive venue lies in the state or federal courts located in `[COUNTY], New Jersey`.

**9.2 Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by email to the addresses below, with delivery confirmation, or by overnight courier.

**9.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties regarding its subject matter.

**9.4 Amendment.** Amendments must be in writing signed by both Parties.

**9.5 Assignment.** Contractor may not assign this Agreement without the Company's written consent. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor in interest by merger, sale, or reorganization.

**9.6 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
