# Operating Agreement of `[LLC NAME]`

**A New Jersey Single-Member Limited Liability Company**

This Operating Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into and effective as of `[FORMATION DATE]` (the "**Effective Date**") by `[FOUNDER NAME]`, an individual residing at `[FOUNDER ADDRESS]` (the "**Member**"), as the sole member of `[LLC NAME]`, a New Jersey limited liability company (the "**Company**").

The Member is forming the Company under the New Jersey Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, **N.J.S.A. 42:2C-1 et seq.** (the "**Act**"), and intends, by this Agreement, to set forth the terms governing the Company's existence, governance, and operations.

---

## Article 1 — Formation

**1.1 Formation.** The Company was formed on `[FORMATION DATE]` upon the filing of the Public Records Filing for New Business Entity with the New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services.

**1.2 Name.** The name of the Company is `[LLC NAME]`. The Member may change the name by filing an amendment with the Division of Revenue.

**1.3 Principal Office.** The principal office of the Company is `[PRINCIPAL OFFICE ADDRESS]`, or such other place as the Member designates.

**1.4 Registered Agent.** The Company's registered agent is `[REGISTERED AGENT NAME]`, with a registered office at `[REGISTERED AGENT ADDRESS]`.

**1.5 Purpose.** The Company is formed to engage in any lawful business permitted under the Act, including without limitation the development, marketing, and operation of software-as-a-service products for the intake, processing, classification, and management of documents (the "**Business**").

**1.6 Term.** The Company's existence is perpetual unless dissolved in accordance with this Agreement or the Act.

## Article 2 — Member; Capital

**2.1 Sole Member.** The sole member of the Company is `[FOUNDER NAME]`, owning **one hundred percent (100%)** of the membership interests (the "**Interest**").

**2.2 Initial Capital Contribution.** On or about the Effective Date, the Member contributed to the Company the assets listed on **Schedule A** (which may include cash, equipment, software, source code, prior drafts, designs, domain names, brand assets, and other intangible property associated with the Business). The Member's initial capital account is set at the agreed-upon value of those contributions.

**2.3 Additional Contributions.** The Member is not obligated to make additional capital contributions, but may do so at any time and shall record any such contribution on Schedule A.

**2.4 No Third-Party Beneficiary of Contributions.** No creditor of the Company has any right to require the Member to make any contribution.

**2.5 No Interest on Contributions.** The Member is not entitled to interest on any capital contribution.

## Article 3 — Distributions and Allocations

**3.1 Distributions.** The Member may cause the Company to distribute available cash or other property to the Member at any time, subject only to the solvency limitation in **N.J.S.A. 42:2C-34** (no distribution if it would render the Company unable to pay its debts as they become due, or if the Company's total assets would be less than the sum of its total liabilities).

**3.2 Allocations.** Because the Company has a single member, all items of income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit are allocated 100% to the Member.

**3.3 Tax Status.** The Company is, by default, a "disregarded entity" for federal income tax purposes under Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-3, and the Member shall report the Company's items of income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit on the Member's individual federal income tax return (Schedule C, E, or F as applicable). The Member may elect S-corporation or C-corporation taxation by timely filing IRS Form 2553 or Form 8832.

## Article 4 — Management

**4.1 Member-Managed.** The Company is **member-managed**. The Member has full, exclusive, and complete authority, power, and discretion to manage and control the business, affairs, and properties of the Company, to make all decisions regarding those matters, and to perform any and all other acts or activities customary or incident to the management of the Company's business.

**4.2 Authority to Bind.** The Member is the sole authorized signatory and agent of the Company and may execute any contract, instrument, or other document on behalf of the Company.

**4.3 Officers (Optional).** The Member may, by written resolution, designate one or more officers (e.g., Chief Executive Officer, President, Treasurer) and delegate specified duties to them. Any such designation does not relieve the Member of the Member's authority or responsibility under this Agreement.

**4.4 Compensation.** The Member is entitled to reasonable compensation for services rendered to the Company, in such amount as the Member determines, plus reimbursement of expenses reasonably incurred on the Company's behalf.

## Article 5 — Books, Records, and Accounting

**5.1 Books.** The Company shall maintain accurate and complete books of account, separate from the Member's personal records, at the principal office.

**5.2 Fiscal Year.** The Company's fiscal year is the calendar year unless the Member elects otherwise consistent with applicable tax law.

**5.3 Bank Accounts.** All Company funds shall be deposited in one or more accounts in the Company's name. The Member shall not commingle Company funds with the Member's personal funds. **Commingling is the single most common reason a New Jersey court will pierce the corporate veil of a single-member LLC.**

## Article 6 — Liability, Indemnification, and Limited Recourse

**6.1 Limitation of Liability.** Except as required by the Act, the Member is not personally liable, solely by reason of being a member, for any debt, obligation, or liability of the Company, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise (N.J.S.A. 42:2C-30).

**6.2 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Member, and any officer designated under § 4.3, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act, against any loss, damage, claim, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) incurred in connection with any act or omission performed or omitted in good faith on behalf of the Company, except for acts or omissions constituting fraud, intentional misconduct, or a knowing violation of law.

**6.3 Advancement of Expenses.** The Company shall advance reasonable expenses incurred by an indemnified person in defending any proceeding upon receipt of a written undertaking to repay the advancement if it is ultimately determined that the person is not entitled to indemnification.

**6.4 Insurance.** The Company may purchase and maintain insurance, including but not limited to errors-and-omissions, cyber-liability, and general commercial liability insurance, on behalf of any person who is or was a Member, officer, employee, or agent of the Company.

## Article 7 — Intellectual Property

**7.1 Company-Owned IP.** All inventions, software, source code, designs, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, know-how, data, and other intellectual property created or acquired by the Company, or by the Member in the course of the Business, are the exclusive property of the Company.

**7.2 Member's Pre-Formation Work.** The Member has separately executed an Intellectual Property Assignment ([`../ip/03-ip-assignment-founder-to-llc.md`](../ip/03-ip-assignment-founder-to-llc.md)) assigning to the Company all intellectual property created by the Member prior to the Effective Date that relates to the Business, including without limitation the IntakeClean software, the multi-layer document AI pipeline, and all associated brand assets.

**7.3 No License to Member.** Nothing in this Agreement grants the Member any personal license, right, or interest in any Company intellectual property other than the Member's right to use that intellectual property in the course of managing the Company.

## Article 8 — Transfers; Admission of New Members

**8.1 No Transfer Without Consent.** The Member shall not transfer, assign, pledge, or encumber any portion of the Member's Interest without the Member's own written consent (which the Member may give freely as the sole owner). This provision is preserved so that, if the Company ever has more than one member, transfer restrictions can be added by amendment without re-executing the entire Agreement.

**8.2 Admission of New Members.** A new member may be admitted to the Company only upon (a) the existing Member's written consent, (b) the new member's written agreement to be bound by this Agreement (as amended), and (c) any required capital contribution.

**8.3 Conversion to Multi-Member.** Upon the admission of a new member, the Member shall amend this Agreement to add multi-member provisions (e.g., voting, distributions, transfer restrictions, drag-along/tag-along rights). Until that amendment is executed, the prior Member's Interest is automatically diluted pro rata to reflect the admitted member's contribution.

## Article 9 — Dissolution

**9.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon the earliest of:
   (a) the Member's written election to dissolve;
   (b) the entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under N.J.S.A. 42:2C-49; or
   (c) any other event causing dissolution under the Act.

**9.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Member shall wind up the Company's affairs, including (i) collecting receivables, (ii) selling non-cash assets, (iii) paying or providing for liabilities, and (iv) distributing any remainder to the Member.

**9.3 Certificate of Cancellation.** Upon completion of winding up, the Member shall file a Certificate of Cancellation with the New Jersey Division of Revenue.

## Article 10 — General Provisions

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**10.2 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to render it valid and enforceable.

**10.3 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by the Member.

**10.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the Member regarding the subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, written or oral.

**10.5 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature (e.g., DocuSign), each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**10.6 Notices.** Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the Member at the address listed in the preamble (or to such other address as the Member designates by written notice).
