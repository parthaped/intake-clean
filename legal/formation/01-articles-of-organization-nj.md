# Public Records Filing for New Business Entity (New Jersey LLC)

> **How to use this file.** New Jersey does not accept paper "Articles of Organization." You file the *Public Records Filing for New Business Entity* online at <https://business.nj.gov/pages/register>. This document is a **worksheet** — fill it in, then transcribe the values into the online form. Save the receipt and the filed Certificate of Formation in `legal/signed/`.
>
> Statutory authority: **N.J.S.A. 42:2C-1 et seq.** (New Jersey Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, "RULLCA").
> Filing fee at time of drafting: **$125** (verify current fee at the link above before submitting).

---

## 1. Business Name

**Proposed name:** `[LLC NAME]`

Requirements (N.J.S.A. 42:2C-8):
- Must contain `Limited Liability Company`, `LLC`, or `L.L.C.`.
- Must be distinguishable from any existing NJ entity. Pre-clear at <https://www.njportal.com/DOR/BusinessNameSearch>.
- Must not falsely imply governmental affiliation, banking, or insurance.

Optional name reservation (UNJ-1) — $50 for 120 days — only if you need to lock the name before filing.

## 2. Business Type

`Domestic Limited Liability Company` (NJ-formed).

## 3. NAICS Code

If unsure, the NJ system will let you pick from a list during filing.

## 4. Effective Date

`[FORMATION DATE]` — typically the date of filing. NJ allows you to specify a future effective date up to 90 days out (useful for January 1 starts to simplify tax-year accounting).

## 5. Duration

## 6. Business Purpose

(Keep it broad. A narrow purpose clause locks you out of pivots.)

## After filing — same-day checklist

- [ ] Save the filed **Certificate of Formation** PDF as `legal/signed/certificate-of-formation.pdf`.
- [ ] Apply for an **EIN** at <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online>. Free, immediate.
- [ ] Within 60 days: file **Form NJ-REG** at <https://www.njportal.com/DOR/BusinessRegistration> for tax registration.
- [ ] Sign [`02-operating-agreement-single-member-nj.md`](02-operating-agreement-single-member-nj.md).
- [ ] Sign [`../ip/03-ip-assignment-founder-to-llc.md`](../ip/03-ip-assignment-founder-to-llc.md) **before** filing the patent.
- [ ] Open a business bank account using the EIN + Certificate of Formation.
- [ ] Calendar the **Annual Report** (due each year by the end of your formation anniversary month, $75).
